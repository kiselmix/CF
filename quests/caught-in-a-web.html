Caught in a Web

Act 1
Reward: 1 Talent Point
A monstrous spider haunts the mountains, trapping the unwary in its web. End the creature before it claims more lives.
How to complete
1. Step 1 — Go to Weaver's Nest
Weaver's Nest is located in The Veiled Passage.
2. Step 2 — Kill the Weaver
Inside the dungeon, look for a huge spider and kill it.
3. Step 3 — Talk to Escaped Prisoner in Castaway's Landing
