The Breach Engine

Act 1
Reward: 1 Talent Point; Spell Crest
Want to leave Prison Island? If you help free my client from Shadowgate Prison, you'll have a place on my ship. To break in, you'll need my Breach Engine, which has been stolen by raiders. I tracked them to Putrid Lake, but I dare not enter the Rat Burrow.
How to complete
1. Step 1 — Go to Putrid Lake
We go to the Blackwater Creek location and go to Putrid Lake.
2. Step 2 — Find Rat Burrow in Putrid Lake
In the Putrid Lake location, look for a cave with a Rat Burrow. Enter Rat Burrow-Cave.
3. Step 3 — Defeat the Raglord
Defeat Raglord and pick up Breach Claw
4. Step 4 — Speak to Shipmaster in encampment
Return through the portal to Castaway's Landing and talk to the Shipmaster.
