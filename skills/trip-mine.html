Trip Mine

Mine AoE Physical
Place a trip mine at a target location. When triggered by an enemy the mine explodes dealing damage in an area around it.
  • Skill Level: 1
  • Cost: Aether 25
  • Physical damage: 6 - 10
  • AoE radius: 2.6
  • Cast speed: 1.5
  • Range: 15
  • Item Level: 55
  • Requirements: Level 18
  • +45(30–50)% Knockback Chance
  • Gain +18(15–25)% of Damage as Extra Physical Damage per 1 Skill Level
  • +2 to Maximum Mines
