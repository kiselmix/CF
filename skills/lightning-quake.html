Lightning Quake

AoEMeleeLightning
Slam the ground with lightning and watch as enemies within the targeted area are damaged by periodic quakes.
  • Skill Level: 1
  • Cost: Aether 25
  • AoE Radius: 4.7
  • Duration: 3
  • Damage Rate: 1
  • Range: 15
  • Requirements: Level 10, 2h Mace or Staff
  • +50% of outgoing Physical Damage Converted to Lightning Damage
  • 20% decreased Attack Speed
  • Gain +7% of Damage as Extra Lightning Damage per 1 Skill Level
